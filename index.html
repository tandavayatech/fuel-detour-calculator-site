Know before you detour

A free, no-account, no-tracking app for UK drivers: work out whether a cheaper fuel stop is actually worth the extra miles, track your real fuel economy per vehicle, and find nearby stations by grade and price.

Detour Calculator

Enter the extra distance and both fuel prices — get the minimum litres you'd need to buy for the detour to pay off, how that compares with your largest fill-up, and what you'd save or pay extra.

⛽

Fuel Log & Stats

Log every fill-up per vehicle and get real MPG, L/100km, cost, and range figures — with honest handling of missed or partial fill-ups.

📍

Nearby Prices

Search by postcode or GPS and compare UK station prices by exact grade — Unleaded, Super Unleaded, Diesel, or Premium Diesel. View them on a map, slide to show only the cheapest, and navigate with the app of your choice.

Fuel Detour Calculator stores everything on your device — there's no account, no ads, and no analytics. See the privacy policy for the full detail, or the guide for a tab-by-tab walkthrough.

Fuel price data comes from the UK Government's Fuel Finder service.
