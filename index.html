Know before you detour

A free, no-account, no-tracking app for UK drivers: work out whether a cheaper fuel stop is actually worth the extra miles, track your real fuel economy per vehicle, and find nearby stations by grade and price.

Get it on Google Play

Detour Calculator

Is the detour worth the cheaper fuel?

Enter how much extra distance the detour adds and both fuel prices. You'll see the minimum litres to buy for it to pay off, how that compares with your largest fill-up so far, and whether you'd save money or pay extra.

Fuel Log & Stats

What's your real fuel economy?

Log your fill-ups (works with multiple vehicles) and get real fuel economy (MPG or L/100km), cost, and range figures — with charts of your economy and spending over time. Handles forgotten or partial fill-ups. Back up or move your logs with one-tap export and import.

Fuel Prices

Find cheaper fuel!

Search by current location or postcode and compare UK station prices by fuel grade. See the price, the distance, and when each station is open. View them on a map, and navigate to your chosen fuel station with the app of your choice.

Fuel Detour Calculator stores everything on your device — there's no account, no ads, and no analytics. See the privacy policy for the full detail, or the guide for a walkthrough.

Fuel price data and opening hours come from the UK Government's Fuel Finder service.
