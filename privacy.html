Privacy Policy

Last updated: 13 September 2026 Developer: Tandavaya Tech

Fuel Detour Calculator is a fuel-economy and fuel-price tool. This page explains, plainly, what the app does and doesn't do with your data.

At a glance
✓

No account or sign-in

✓

Your fuel logs stay on your device only

✕

No ads, analytics, or tracking SDKs

✕

We don't run a server — there's nowhere to send your data even if we wanted to

What's stored on your device

Vehicle names, and every fuel refill you log — odometer reading, litres, price, date, and the two tracking flags (Missed previous refill and Not filled to brim) — are saved to the app's private storage on your phone. No other app can read this data, and we never see it: there's no server this app talks to for your log data.

If your phone's standard Android backup is switched on, this data may be included in that backup like any other app's files — that backup is tied to your own Google account, not to us. Uninstalling the app, or deleting entries within it, removes the data permanently. Exporting to CSV creates a file entirely under your control; where it goes after that is up to you.

Location and postcode searches

The Prices tab is the only part of the app that involves your location, and only when you actively search. There are two ways to search:

  • Type a postcode — it's converted to a map coordinate using Android's built-in Geocoder.
  • Leave it blank — the app asks permission to use your device's GPS location via Google Play services.

Your coordinates are used entirely on your device to work out which stations count as "nearby" — they are never sent to the government's Fuel Finder service. That service only ever receives a request for a page of its full nationwide station list; matching that data against your location happens locally in the app afterwards. The only place your location data is sent off your device is Google — to resolve a typed postcode into coordinates, and, if you switch to the map view, to render the map centred on your position. This goes straight from your device to Google — never through us. Search results are kept in memory only while the app is open, for up to 15 minutes, then discarded; they are never written to disk.

What we don't do

  • No account creation or sign-in of any kind
  • No advertising or ad networks
  • No analytics or crash-reporting SDKs
  • No selling or sharing of data for marketing purposes
  • No knowing collection of data from children — the app isn't directed at children

Third-party services

Google Play services (Location and Maps) — used to resolve GPS/postcode coordinates and display the results on a map. Governed by Google's Privacy Policy.

UK Government Fuel Finder service — supplies the fuel station and price data shown in the app. Your location is never sent to this service; the app requests pages of the full national dataset and filters them by distance on your device. Governed by the GOV.UK privacy notice.

Your choices

  • Decline the location permission and use postcode search instead — or skip the Prices tab entirely; the rest of the app works fully offline.
  • Export your fuel log to a CSV file at any time from the Fuel Log tab.
  • Uninstall the app to remove all locally stored data immediately.
