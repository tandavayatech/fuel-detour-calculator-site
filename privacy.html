Privacy Policy

Last updated: 23 September 2026 Developer: Tandavaya Tech

Fuel Detour Calculator is a fuel-economy and fuel-price tool. This page explains, plainly, what the app does and doesn't do with your data.

At a glance
✓

No account or sign-in

✓

Your fuel logs stay on your device only

✕

No ads, analytics, or tracking SDKs

✓

A Prices search sends your location to our own server, briefly, to find nearby stations — never your fuel log

What's stored on your device

Vehicle names, and every fuel refill you log — odometer reading, litres, price, date, and the two tracking flags (Missed previous refill and Not filled to brim) — are saved to the app's private storage on your phone. The app also remembers what you last typed into the Calculator (distances, efficiency and prices) and your settings — efficiency unit, Light/Dark choice, and whether you've seen the welcome tour — so it's ready when you return. No other app can read this data, and it never leaves your device: it isn't sent to our server (described below) or anywhere else, ever.

If your phone's standard Android backup is switched on, this data may be included in that backup like any other app's files — that backup is tied to your own Google account, not to us. Uninstalling the app, or deleting entries within it, removes the data permanently. Exporting to CSV creates a file entirely under your control — it can include your vehicle names and every logged entry, for one vehicle or all of them — and where it goes after that is up to you. Importing a CSV only reads the file you choose.

Location and postcode searches

The Prices tab is the only part of the app that involves your location, and only when you actively search. There are two ways to search:

  • Type a postcode — it's converted to a map coordinate using Android's built-in Geocoder.
  • Leave it blank — the app asks permission to use your device's GPS location via Google Play services.

Those coordinates are sent to our own backend server, which we run to find nearby stations for you. The server uses them for that one search only — to work out which stations count as "nearby" — and doesn't save them, build a history of your searches, or link them to any account (the app doesn't have accounts). Like any web server, our hosting provider's standard operational logs may briefly retain technical details of each request, such as the coordinates and your device's IP address, as ordinary infrastructure logging — this isn't something our app code does deliberately, isn't reviewed or analysed by us, and is retained only for a limited period under our hosting provider's standard practices, not indefinitely.

Your coordinates are never sent to the government's Fuel Finder service. Our server downloads that service's full nationwide station and price list itself, and works out which stations are near you on our side — Fuel Finder only ever receives a request for a page of its full dataset, with no location information in it at all.

The only place your location data is sent directly from your device, without going through us first, is Google — to resolve a typed postcode into coordinates, and, if you switch to the map view, to render the map centred on your position.

To avoid downloading the same data repeatedly, both the app and our server keep the fuel station and price data cached for a short time — station details for up to an hour, prices for up to 15 minutes — then refresh it. This is shared station/price reference data, not anything about you, and is never written to disk on your device.

If you tap the Navigate button on a station, the app passes that station's location — not yours — to the navigation app you choose, such as Google Maps or Waze. What that app does with it is governed by its own privacy policy.

What we don't do

  • No account creation or sign-in of any kind
  • No advertising or ad networks
  • No analytics or crash-reporting SDKs
  • No selling or sharing of data for marketing purposes
  • No knowing collection of data from children — the app isn't directed at children

Third-party services

Google Play services (Location and Maps) — used to resolve GPS/postcode coordinates and display the results on a map. Governed by Google's Privacy Policy.

Navigation apps — only if you tap Navigate, and only the app you choose. It receives the station's location, not yours. Governed by that app's own privacy policy.

UK Government Fuel Finder service — supplies the fuel station and price data shown in the app. Your location is never sent to this service; our own server requests pages of the full national dataset and filters them by distance itself. Governed by the GOV.UK privacy notice.

Our backend server — runs on Google Cloud, which provides the hosting infrastructure. Google doesn't use this data for its own purposes; it's simply where our server runs, the same way any website needs somewhere to be hosted. Governed by Google's Privacy Policy for that hosting relationship.

Your choices

  • Decline the location permission and use postcode search instead — or skip the Prices tab entirely; the rest of the app works fully offline.
  • Export your fuel log to a CSV file at any time from the Fuel Log tab.
  • Uninstall the app to remove all locally stored data immediately.
